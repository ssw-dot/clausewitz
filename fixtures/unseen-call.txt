Riverside Community Arts Fund 2026 -- Call for Applications

About. The Fund supports small arts and heritage projects run by community
organisations. Grants of up to $6,000 are available. There is no application
fee, and no matching contribution is required.

Who may apply. Applications are welcome from registered nonprofit
organisations, charities and community groups of any size.

Award ceremony. Shortlisted applicants must attend the award ceremony in
Toronto in person to receive their grant.

Timeline. Applications close on 30 November 2026. Decisions are announced
within eight weeks of closing.
